Frequently Asked Questions

You can check your order status from My Orders. Open an order to see its current tracking status.
We currently support UPI, Credit / Debit Card and Cash on Delivery.
Go to My Orders and open your order. You can see the current delivery status and tracking progress there.
If your order is still eligible for cancellation, you can use the Cancel Order option from the order details page.
For return or replacement assistance, please contact our support team with your order ID and product details.
Refund processing time can depend on the payment method and the applicable return/refund process. Contact support if you need help with a specific order.
Go to My Address from the dashboard to add or manage your saved delivery addresses.
You can review a product after its order has been successfully delivered. Open the Reviews section from your dashboard.
Open My Profile from the dashboard and update your available account details.
